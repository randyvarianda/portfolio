Discovery

Context

Of 9.2 million merchants on Tokopedia, only 6.1% transact regularly each month, and 80% of transactions come from just 0.7% of sellers. A key driver of this gap is shop trustworthiness. When making a purchase, buyers consistently rank shop quality as the most important factor, currently signaled through Reputation Badges and Reviews.

Benefit

Working with the product and data teams, we identified the core benefits of introducing a unified shop quality standard:

Problem

Tokopedia needed a single, fair standard to assess merchant quality and build buyer trust. We proposed a new Shop Score to better distinguish high-quality merchants from the broader pool, with a higher score indicating a more reliable and trustworthy seller.

Proposed Solution

Revamping seller performance assessment into a unified standard would build consistent trustworthiness across the platform and give buyers a clearer signal when choosing where to shop.

Expected Impact

  • Provide fair and single currency performance assessment for seller
  • Provide single currency for Tokopedia to find high quality sellers
  • Re-establish Power Merchant as the single credibility signal for buyers, while filtering out underperforming sellers to strengthen its exclusivity and perceived value.
