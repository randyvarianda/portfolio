Overview

Company

Office Coffee is more than a cafe. It is a staple for the professional community in Banjarmasin, known for its modern-industrial atmosphere and work-from-cafe culture. The space faced a familiar challenge: keeping the guest experience seamless during peak hours. This project focused on designing a dine-in app to connect physical comfort with digital convenience.

My Role

As Product Designer, I led this project end to end. My responsibilities included competitive and user research, concept exploration and prototyping, UX/UI and interaction design, user flow mapping, and cross-functional collaboration with engineering and the PM.

Design

Dine in order from mobile apps

Guests can browse the full menu, customize their order, and place it directly from their phone without waiting for staff. Designed for speed and clarity during busy hours.

Seamless Payment

Multiple payment methods supported in a single, frictionless flow. Guests can pay instantly without juggling cash or waiting for a bill.

Track Dine-In Transaction

Guests can follow their order status in real time from placement to preparation. Reduces anxiety and removes the need to flag down staff for updates.

Loyalty Point

Every order earns points that can be redeemed on future visits. A simple way to reward regulars and encourage repeat visits.

Membership Office Coffee

Tiered membership with exclusive perks for frequent guests. Designed to deepen loyalty and give top customers a reason to keep coming back.
